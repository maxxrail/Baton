# Managing your account and password

## Resetting your password

Click "Forgot password" on the sign-in page and we'll email you a reset link that works for
1 hour. If it doesn't arrive, check your spam folder.

## Changing your email address

Sign in and update it under Account > Profile. We send a confirmation link to the new address;
the change takes effect when you click it.

## Guest checkout

You can check out as a guest. To see guest orders in an account later, create an account with
the same email address.

## Deleting your account

Contact support to delete your account. We remove your personal data within 30 days, except
order records we must keep for tax purposes. Unused loyalty points are lost when the account
is deleted.
