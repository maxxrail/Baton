# Contacting customer support

## Support hours

Our support team is available Monday to Friday, 8 a.m.-8 p.m. Eastern, and Saturday,
10 a.m.-4 p.m. Eastern. We're closed on Sundays and US federal holidays. Our virtual assistant
answers chat messages any time.

## Ways to reach us

- Chat: the chat bubble on any page of our website.
- Email: support@northwind.example. We reply within one business day.
- Text: (555) 010-4400.

We don't have a phone line for voice calls.

## Our company

Northwind Goods is an online-only store; we don't have retail locations or a showroom.
