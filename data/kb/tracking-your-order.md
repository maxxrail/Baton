# Tracking your order

## Tracking emails

As soon as your order ships we email you a tracking number and a link to the carrier's
tracking page. Tracking can take up to 24 hours to show the first scan.

## Carriers we use

US orders ship with UPS or USPS depending on size and destination. Canadian orders ship with
Canada Post once they cross the border.

## My tracking hasn't updated

It's normal for tracking to pause for 2-3 days while a package moves between hubs. If there's
been no update for 5 business days, contact us and we'll open a trace with the carrier.

## Marked delivered but not received

Check around your door, with neighbours and your building's mail room, and wait one business
day: carriers sometimes mark packages delivered early. If it still hasn't turned up, contact
us within 14 days of the delivery date and we'll investigate and send a replacement or refund
once the carrier's investigation is complete.
