# Damaged, defective or wrong items

## Report it within 7 days

If something arrives broken, faulty, or isn't what you ordered, contact us within **7 days of
delivery**. Please include your order number and a photo of the item and the packaging.

## What we'll do

We'll send a free replacement, or a full refund if the item is out of stock or you prefer
one. You don't pay return shipping for damaged, defective or wrong items, and for items under
$25 we usually won't ask you to send them back at all.

## Problems after 7 days

A product that stops working after the first week may be covered by our warranty. See
"Product warranty".
