# Product warranty

## What's covered

| Product | Warranty |
|---|---|
| Cookware (pans, Dutch ovens, cast iron) | Lifetime warranty against manufacturing defects |
| Kitchen knives | 5 years |
| Small appliances (kettles, grinders, stand mixers) | 2 years |
| Linens, towels and textiles | 1 year |
| Everything else | 1 year |

The warranty starts on the delivery date and covers defects in materials and workmanship.

## What isn't covered

Normal wear, scratches, discolouration, damage from misuse (for example overheating an empty
pan, or putting a knife in the dishwasher), and products bought from other retailers.

## How to make a warranty claim

Contact support with your order number, a description of the problem and photos. If the claim
is approved we repair or replace the item; if it's discontinued we offer a comparable product
or store credit.

## Registering your product

You don't need to register a product to be covered: your order record is your proof of
purchase. If the product was a gift, the warranty still applies; contact us with the gift
sender's order number or the packing slip.

## Replacement parts

We sell replacement lids, handles, gaskets and kettle bases for current products. Contact
support with the product name and we'll check availability. Replacement parts ordered under an
approved warranty claim are free.

## Warranty and returns

The return window and the warranty are separate. Within 30 days of delivery you can return an
unused item for a refund; after that, defects are handled under the warranty with a repair or
replacement rather than a refund.
