# Return policy

## Return window

You can return most items within **30 days of delivery** for a refund to the original payment
method. This 30-day window replaces our previous 60-day policy for all orders delivered from
February 1, 2026.

## What can be returned

Items must be unused, in their original packaging, with all parts and accessories. These
can't be returned: opened food and drink (coffee, tea), opened bath products, candles that
have been burned, gift cards, and items marked "final sale".

## How to start a return

Contact support with your order number. We'll check the order and create a return
authorization (RMA), then email a return label and instructions. Please don't send items back
without an RMA number, because we can't match them to your order.

## Return shipping cost

Return shipping is free for defective or incorrect items. For other returns, a $6.95 return
shipping fee is deducted from the refund.

## After we receive your return

We inspect returns within 3 business days of arrival and then issue the refund. See "When will
I get my refund?" for how long the money takes to appear.

## Returning a gift

Gift recipients can return an item within 30 days of delivery without telling the person who
sent it. Contact support with the order number from the packing slip; the refund is issued as
a Northwind gift card emailed to the recipient instead of going back to the purchaser's card.

## Sets and bundles

Items bought as a set or bundle (for example a 3-piece cookware set or a towel bundle) must be
returned complete. We can't refund part of a bundle, because the bundle price is lower than the
items' individual prices.

## Final sale items

Items marked "final sale" on the product page can't be returned or exchanged unless they arrive
damaged or defective. Final sale items are clearly labelled before checkout and in the order
confirmation email.

## Items returned without an RMA or after the window

Returns that arrive without an RMA number or after the return window can't be refunded. We hold
them for 30 days so you can arrange to have them sent back to you at your cost; after that they
are donated.

## Refund amount

The refund covers the price you paid for the returned items, minus the return shipping fee where
it applies. Original shipping charges are refunded only if the whole order is returned because
it was damaged, defective or wrong.
