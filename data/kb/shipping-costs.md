# Shipping costs and free shipping

This article replaces our earlier free shipping offer. The current rates apply to all orders
placed from June 15, 2026.

## Free standard shipping on orders over $75

US orders with a subtotal of **$75 or more** (after discounts, before tax) ship free with
standard shipping.

## Rates for orders under $75

| Method | Cost |
|---|---|
| Standard (3-7 business days) | $6.95 |
| Expedited (2 business days) | $14.95 |

Expedited shipping is never free, even on orders over $75. Subscription shipments always ship
free.

## Oversized items

Cast-iron sets and stand mixers carry an extra $10 handling fee because of their weight.
