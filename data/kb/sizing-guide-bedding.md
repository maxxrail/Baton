# Sizing guide for sheets, duvet covers and towels

## Sheets

| Size | Flat sheet | Fitted sheet | Fits mattress up to |
|---|---|---|---|
| Twin | 66 x 96 in | 39 x 75 in | 14 in deep |
| Full | 81 x 96 in | 54 x 75 in | 14 in deep |
| Queen | 90 x 102 in | 60 x 80 in | 16 in deep |
| King | 108 x 102 in | 76 x 80 in | 16 in deep |
| California King | 108 x 102 in | 72 x 84 in | 16 in deep |

Our "deep pocket" fitted sheets fit mattresses up to 18 inches deep.

## Duvet covers

Duvet covers are cut 2 inches larger than the matching insert: Full/Queen covers are
90 x 92 in and King/California King covers are 106 x 92 in.

## Towels

Bath towels are 30 x 56 in, bath sheets 35 x 70 in, hand towels 16 x 30 in and washcloths
13 x 13 in.

## Shrinkage

Our cotton and linen are pre-washed, so expect less than 3% shrinkage if you follow the care
instructions.
