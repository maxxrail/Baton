# When will I get my refund?

## Where refunds go

Refunds always go back to the original payment method. We can't refund to a different card,
to a bank account, or as cash.

## How long it takes

Once we issue a refund it usually appears within:

| Payment method | Time to appear |
|---|---|
| Credit or debit card | 5-10 business days |
| PayPal | 3-5 business days |
| Apple Pay / Google Pay | 5-10 business days (same as the card behind it) |
| Gift card | Immediately, back onto the gift card |

The time depends on your bank, not on us. Your statement may show the refund as a reversal of
the original charge.

## Orders paid partly with a gift card

The gift card portion goes back to the gift card first; the rest goes back to your card.

## Refunds for cancelled orders

Cancelling an order that hasn't shipped refunds the full amount charged.
