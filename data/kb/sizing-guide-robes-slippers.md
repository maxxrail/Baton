# Sizing guide for robes and slippers

## Robes

Our waffle and terry robes are unisex with a relaxed fit.

| Size | Chest | Length |
|---|---|---|
| S/M | up to 42 in | 44 in |
| L/XL | up to 50 in | 46 in |
| 2XL/3XL | up to 58 in | 48 in |

If you're between sizes, choose the smaller one for a closer fit or the larger for a roomy fit.

## Slippers

| Size | US women's | US men's |
|---|---|---|
| S | 5-6 | 4-5 |
| M | 7-8 | 6-7 |
| L | 9-10 | 8-9 |
| XL | 11-12 | 10-11 |

The felt lining packs down a little with wear, so slippers that feel snug at first will loosen.
