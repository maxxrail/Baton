# Gift cards

## Buying a gift card

Northwind gift cards are digital and come in amounts from $25 to $500. They're emailed to the
recipient on the date you choose, with your message.

## Expiry and fees

Gift cards never expire and have no fees.

## Using a gift card

Enter the 16-character code at checkout. Any balance left stays on the card for next time. If
the gift card doesn't cover the whole order, pay the rest with another payment method.

## Restrictions

Gift cards can't be redeemed for cash (except where the law requires it), can't be used to buy
other gift cards, and can't be returned or refunded.

## Lost gift card codes

Contact support with the purchaser's order number and we'll resend the code to the
recipient's email.
