# Caring for cast-iron cookware

## Cleaning

Clean cast iron while it's still warm with hot water and a brush or chain-mail scrubber. A
little mild dish soap is fine. Don't soak it and never put it in the dishwasher.

## Drying and oiling

Dry the pan completely on a low burner, then rub a thin layer of neutral oil over the cooking
surface. Wipe off the excess so it doesn't turn sticky.

## Re-seasoning

If you see rust or dull grey patches, scrub them off, coat the whole pan in a thin layer of
oil, and bake it upside down at 450°F for one hour.

## Enamelled cast iron

Our enamelled Dutch ovens don't need seasoning. Avoid metal utensils and heating them empty,
which can crack the enamel. They are dishwasher-safe, but hand washing keeps the enamel
glossy for longer.
