# Redeeming Northwind Rewards points

## What points are worth

Every **100 points = $5 off** an order. You can redeem points in blocks of 100 at checkout,
up to 50% of the order subtotal.

## What you can't do with points

Points can't be exchanged for cash, transferred to another account, or used to buy gift
cards.

## Do points expire?

Points expire after 18 months with no account activity. Any purchase or redemption resets the
clock.

## Combining points with promo codes

You can use points and one promo code on the same order.
