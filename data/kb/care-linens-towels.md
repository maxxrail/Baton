# Caring for sheets, linens and towels

## Washing

Machine wash in warm water (up to 40°C / 104°F) on a gentle cycle with a mild detergent. Wash
dark colours separately the first few times.

## Avoid

Don't use bleach, fabric softener or dryer sheets: softener coats the fibres and makes towels
less absorbent.

## Drying

Tumble dry on low and remove promptly to limit wrinkles, or line dry. Linen softens with every
wash.

## Ironing

Iron cotton sateen on medium heat while slightly damp. Linen can be ironed on high, but most
people prefer it relaxed and unironed.
