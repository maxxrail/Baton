# Coffee, tea and refill subscriptions FAQ

## What can I subscribe to?

Coffee, loose-leaf tea, hand soap refills and candles, delivered every 2, 4 or 8 weeks.
Subscription shipments always ship free.

## Pausing, resuming and cancelling

You can pause, resume or cancel a subscription at any time, with no fees. When you resume a
paused subscription, the next shipment is scheduled one delivery cycle from the day you resume.

## Is there a minimum commitment?

No. There's no minimum number of shipments.

## Refunds for subscription shipments

Cancelling stops future shipments. Shipments that were already sent aren't refunded, but you can
return unopened items within the normal return window.

## Changing what's in my subscription

You can change the product, grind or delivery frequency at any time before the next shipment
is charged.
