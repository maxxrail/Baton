# Payment methods we accept

## Accepted payment methods

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay and
Northwind gift cards.

## Not accepted

We don't accept cash, checks, money orders, cryptocurrency or pay-later services.

## When your card is charged

Your card is authorised when you place the order and charged when it ships. If an order ships
in several packages, you'll see one charge per package.

## Currency

All prices are in US dollars, including for Canadian orders. Your bank sets the exchange rate.

## Payment security

We never see or store your full card number; payments are handled by our PCI-compliant payment
processor. Our support team will never ask for your card number.
