# Shipping times within the United States

Every order ships from our warehouse in Columbus, Ohio. Delivery time is the processing time
(see "How long does it take to process my order?") plus the transit time below.

## Standard shipping (3-7 business days)

| Region | Transit time |
|---|---|
| Midwest (OH, IN, IL, MI, WI, MN, IA, MO, KY) | 2-3 business days |
| East Coast and South | 3-5 business days |
| Mountain states and Texas | 4-6 business days |
| West Coast (CA, OR, WA, NV, AZ) | 5-7 business days |
| Alaska, Hawaii and US territories | 7-12 business days |

## Expedited shipping (2 business days)

Expedited orders placed before 12 p.m. Eastern on a business day leave the warehouse the same
day and arrive in 2 business days anywhere in the contiguous US. Expedited shipping isn't
available to Alaska, Hawaii, US territories or PO boxes.

## Weekends and holidays

Carriers don't count weekends or US federal holidays as business days, and our warehouse is
closed on them too. Orders placed on a Friday afternoon usually leave on Monday.

## PO boxes, APO and FPO addresses

Standard shipping can go to PO boxes and to APO, FPO and DPO military addresses; it uses USPS and
takes 7-14 business days. Expedited shipping and oversized items (cast-iron sets and stand
mixers) can't ship to these addresses.

## Signature on delivery

Orders over $400 need a signature on delivery. If nobody is home, the carrier leaves a notice and
tries again the next business day; after three attempts the package is held at the local carrier
location for 5 days before it's returned to us.

## Delays

Severe weather, carrier backlogs and the holiday peak can add 1-3 business days to the times
above. We email you if your order is held up on our side. Shipping times are estimates, not
guarantees, and we can't refund shipping charges for carrier delays.
