# How subscription billing works

## When you're charged

Each shipment is charged **2 days before it ships**, to the card saved on the subscription. You
get a reminder email 3 days before every charge.

## Skipping one shipment

If you only want to skip the next delivery, pause the subscription and resume it later; the
next shipment is scheduled one cycle after you resume.

## Failed payments

If a charge fails, we retry once a day for 3 days. After that the subscription is paused
automatically until the card is updated.

## Subscriber pricing

Subscribers save 10% on every shipment compared with one-time purchases. The discount can't be
combined with promo codes.
