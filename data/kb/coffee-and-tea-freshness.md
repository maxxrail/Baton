# Coffee and tea freshness and storage

## Roast dates

We roast coffee in small batches every Monday and Thursday and ship it within 3 days of
roasting. The roast date is printed on every bag.

## How long it stays fresh

Whole-bean coffee is best within 4 weeks of the roast date; ground coffee within 2 weeks.
Loose-leaf tea keeps its flavour for about 12 months.

## Storage

Keep coffee and tea in their resealable bags or an airtight container, away from light, heat
and moisture. Don't store coffee in the fridge: it absorbs odours and moisture.

## Grind options

Coffee comes as whole bean or ground for drip, French press or espresso.
