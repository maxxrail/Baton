# Is it dishwasher and microwave safe? Ceramics and glassware care

## Stoneware dinnerware

Our stoneware plates, bowls and mugs are dishwasher, microwave and oven safe up to 400°F.
Avoid sudden temperature changes, such as moving a dish from the freezer to a hot oven.

## Hand-painted and gold-rim pieces

Anything with a gold or platinum rim is hand wash only and not microwave safe: the metal can
spark.

## Glassware

Our borosilicate glassware is dishwasher safe on the top rack and can hold boiling water. Thin
crystal stemware is hand wash only.

## Removing marks

Grey marks from cutlery come off with a paste of baking soda and water.
