# How long does it take to process my order?

## Processing time

We pack and hand most orders to the carrier within **1-2 business days**. During our holiday
peak (mid-November to December 24) processing can take up to 4 business days.

## Order cutoff

Orders placed before 12 p.m. Eastern on a business day count as received that day. Later
orders count from the next business day.

## Orders with several items

If part of an order is out of stock, we ship what's ready and send the rest when it arrives,
at no extra shipping cost. Each package gets its own tracking email.

## Pre-orders

Items marked "pre-order" ship on the date shown on the product page. The rest of the order
waits for them unless you place the pre-order separately.
