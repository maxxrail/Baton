# International orders and shipping to Canada

## Where we ship

We ship to the United States and Canada. We don't ship to other countries yet, and we can't
send orders to freight forwarders.

## How long shipping to Canada takes

Standard shipping to Canada takes **6-10 business days** after your order leaves the
warehouse. Orders to remote areas (Yukon, Northwest Territories, Nunavut) can take up to
15 business days. Expedited shipping isn't available for Canadian addresses.

## Shipping cost to Canada

Shipping to Canada is a flat CA$18 per order. The free shipping threshold applies to US
addresses only.

## Duties and taxes

Prices at checkout are in US dollars. We collect GST/HST and any import duties at checkout, so
there's nothing extra to pay when the package arrives. If a carrier asks you for a duty
payment anyway, don't pay it: contact us and we'll sort it out with the carrier.

## Returns from Canada

Canadian customers can return items within the same window as US customers. We email a
prepaid return label for defective items; for other returns, the customer pays return
shipping.
