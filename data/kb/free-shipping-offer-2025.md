# Free shipping on orders over $50

Good news: all US orders over **$50** now ship free with standard shipping. No code needed;
the discount is applied automatically at checkout.

## What's included

Free shipping covers standard delivery to any US address, including Alaska and Hawaii.
Expedited shipping is $12.95 on every order.

## Orders under $50

Orders under $50 ship for a flat $5.95.
