# Returns and exchanges policy (2024)

## 60-day returns

Not quite right? You can return any unused item within **60 days of delivery** for a full
refund. Return shipping is always free: we include a prepaid label with every order.

## How to return

Use the prepaid label in your package, or contact support for a new one. Refunds are issued
within 5 business days of the return arriving at our warehouse.

## Exceptions

Gift cards and opened food items can't be returned.
