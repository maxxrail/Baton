# Northwind Rewards loyalty program and tiers

## Earning points

Members earn **1 point for every $1** spent on products (shipping and taxes don't earn
points). Points appear on your account when the order ships. Joining is free: every Northwind
account is enrolled automatically.

## Tiers

Your tier is based on lifetime points, the total you've ever earned.

| Tier | Lifetime points | Perks |
|---|---|---|
| Bronze | 0-999 | Earn points, birthday bonus of 50 points |
| Silver | 1,000-2,499 | Everything in Bronze, plus free expedited shipping on orders over $75 |
| Gold | 2,500 and up | Everything in Silver, plus early access to new products and 1.5 points per $1 |

Tiers never go down: once you reach a tier you keep it.

## Returns and points

Points earned on an order are removed if the order is returned or refunded.

## Missing points

If points from an order haven't appeared 3 days after it shipped, contact support and a
teammate will review it.
